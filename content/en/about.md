Ed Barros works at the intersection of engineering and leadership, building
the systems, teams, and practices that let technology organizations ship
with confidence. This site collects his writing on architecture,
engineering management, and the judgment calls in between.

He favors clear structure over cleverness — explicit systems, teams built
for ownership, and decisions grounded in principle rather than habit. The
same approach carries into how he writes: plainly, and without claiming
more authority than the argument earns.

Outside of work, barbecue, jiu-jitsu, and a considered stance on liberty
round things out — three interests that show up in his writing almost as
often as anything technical.
